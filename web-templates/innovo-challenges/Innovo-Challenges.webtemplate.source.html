Every idea submitted to Innovo Labs is answering one of these. They were set by the business, not by the programme — which is why an idea that lands squarely on one moves faster than an idea that lands near none.

{% comment %} ══════════ The seven ══════════ A card grid. Still an
    underneath: these are referred to as "challenge 4" in campaign material, so the order carries information even though the cards are read as a set rather than a sequence. {% endcomment %}

    1. 01

      Modernising Construction Methods

      Innovo still depends on fragmented, labour-intensive delivery methods and needs to shift toward more industrialised, technology-enabled construction to unlock productivity.

    2. 02

      BIM Value Beyond Design Coordination

      BIM is often used too narrowly, for design coordination alone. The challenge is to unlock its full value across construction, operations, asset management and decision-making.

    3. 03

      Reducing Labour Dependency

      Labour shortages, an ageing workforce and rising project complexity make lower dependence on manual labour essential for long-term sustainability.

    4. 04

      Data Intelligence & Forecasting

      Innovo generates enormous amounts of data, but much of it remains disconnected, unused or reactive. The challenge is turning project data into predictive intelligence.

    5. 05

      Accelerating Process Automation

      Workflows still depend on spreadsheets, emails, manual approvals and disconnected systems. Automation aims to digitise and streamline repetitive operational tasks.

    6. 06

      Real-Time Site Operations & Smart Logistics

      Sites are dynamic environments where delays often follow poor visibility into materials, equipment, workforce movement and site conditions. The aim is connected, intelligent jobsites.

    7. 07

      Workforce, Skills and Subcontractor Experience

      Innovo must rethink how it attracts, develops and retains talent, while improving collaboration with the subcontractors who perform most project work.
