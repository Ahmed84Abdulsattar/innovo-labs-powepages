How we measure

Where these numbers come from.

1 · Baselined

Before anything runs, the team records how long the task takes and what it costs today.

2 · Piloted

The initiative runs on a live project, not a demo — measured against that same baseline.

3 · Verified

The business unit signs off the delta before it is counted. Unverified savings are excluded.

4 · Annualised

The verified figure is scaled to a full year at current deployment, and re-checked each quarter.

Figures are cumulative across all in-flight and piloting initiatives, and are refreshed by the Digital Innovation team. Anything still in pre-evaluation is excluded until it has a verified baseline.
